I'm currently a sophomore studying Mechanical Engineering at Villanova University, where I am pursuing a concentration in Thermal-Fluid Systems and a minor in Aerospace Engineering. I am also an Aerodynamics Subsystem Engineer on the Chassis System of NovaRacing, Villanova's Formula SAE team, where I work on aerodynamic design in CAD and help manufacture composite components for the team's race car.

My main interest right now is using simulation and analysis to improve mechanical and aerodynamic designs. I enjoy working in CAD and want to better understand how a design performs beyond its geometry alone. I am especially interested in CFD and using airflow simulation to evaluate designs and support better design decisions.

I'm also interested in composites and advanced materials, particularly materials used in demanding or extreme environments. I want to learn more about how properties such as strength, weight, temperature resistance, and manufacturability affect the design and use of a component. Through Formula SAE, I have gained hands-on experience manufacturing carbon-fiber parts and want to continue expanding my experience with composites, machining, and other manufacturing processes.

As I continue through my ME and AE coursework, I want to build a stronger foundation in fluid mechanics, heat transfer, materials, CAD, and simulation. Long term, I am interested in mechanical design and development work within the aerospace, defense, and high-performance automotive industries, especially projects involving aerodynamics, thermal-fluid systems, advanced materials, and complex mechanical systems.

Where My Curiosity Goes

I am primarily interested in aeronautics and aerodynamic design, with a focus on material behaviors/aerospace applications, composite structures, and design for manufacturing. I am also interested in using analytical tools such as CFD and FEA to guide design decisions and inform iteration.

01Mechanical Design and CAD

Designing parts and assemblies for performance, packaging, strength, and manufacturability.

02Aerodynamics

How airflow interacts with geometry to shape vehicle and component performance.

03Thermal-Fluid Systems

How heat transfer and fluid flow affect mechanical and aerospace systems.

04Composite Materials

Lightweight materials with high strength and stiffness for their weight.

05Extreme-Environment Materials

Materials built for high temperatures, heavy loads, and harsh conditions.

06Advanced Manufacturing

Machining, additive manufacturing, and composites that turn designs into real parts.

07Testing and Validation
